### Sources and model provenance

All energy results in this report come from a single PHPP 10.6 workbook (`261026-Waterman-Home.xlsx`). Building
geometry is based on the mossArchitects drawings and Revit model received on
<Var k="source_files.cad_files_received_date" />, and on the schematic-design drawings and narrative of August 21, 2026.
Climate data for all variants is the <Var k="narrative.climate.weather_station_name" />, built by BLDGTYP from the
[Phius climate data set](https://phius.org/climate-data). Assembly R-values are calculated in
Flixo. CO<sub>2e</sub> emission factors are from the <Var k="narrative.co2.epa_subgrid_name" /> eGRID subregion; source
energy factors are from the EPA ENERGY STAR Portfolio Manager Technical Reference. Phius CORE 2024 targets are from the
Phius Criteria Calculator <Var k="narrative.user_defined.phius_calculator_version" />, for
<Var k="narrative.user_defined.phius_bedrooms" /> bedrooms.

The future climate data was built by BLDGTYP in October 2026 from the ORNL Cross-Model future weather files
(Chowdhury, Li, Stubbings and New, 2023, [Zenodo 10719179](https://zenodo.org/records/10719179), CC-BY-4.0), the
Climate.OneBuilding TMYx 2011 to 2025 file for Pittsburgh International, the Phius climate data set, the ASHRAE
climatic design conditions (2009 to 2025 editions) and the NOAA CRIS county projections. See the
[Future Climate](/future-climate/) page.

The resilience results come from a separate hourly EnergyPlus model following the Phius REVIVE 2024 protocol, and are
not derived from the PHPP workbook.

### Open items and assumptions to confirm

The following were assumed for modeling and should be confirmed by the design team before these results are relied on:

- **Windows.** No product selected. Modeled as a code-level U-0.30 window in all variants; see
  [Window Thermal Comfort](/windows/#window-thermal-comfort).
- **Sloped roof.** Modeled without the above-deck insulation shown in the schematic-design narrative. See the
  [Sloped Roof](/building_envelope/#recommended-assemblies) card.
- **Basement wall insulation.** Modeled on the interior; the narrative places it on the exterior.
- **Foam layers.** Polyiso above the low-slope roof deck and XPS below the slab, against the foam-free goal.
- **Thermal bridges.** Carried as a percentage allowance, reduced step by step across the variants. Brick support,
  the balcony, the bay window, the garage junction and the elevator overrun need detail-level review.
- **Occupancy.** Modeled for <Var k="building.total_num_occupants" /> occupants, from the Phius bedroom count.
- **Mechanical equipment.** Generic air-source heat pumps for heating, cooling and hot water; the choice between the
  air-to-water and air-to-air options in the narrative is open. ERV model and location to be confirmed.
- **Kitchen hood, humidifier, heated slabs and recirculation loop.** See
  [Appliances and Venting](/mechanical/#appliances-and-venting).
- **Solar PV.** Modeled as zero in all variants.

### Units

Passive House modeling is natively metric, and some charts in this report display SI units. Where results are quoted
in the text they have been converted to IP. The two most common conversions:

- 1 kWh/m²·yr = 0.317 kBtu/ft²·yr
- 1 W/m² = 0.317 Btu/hr·ft²
