### Calculation basis

Annual results come from [PHPP 10.6](https://passivehouse.com/04_phpp/04_phpp.htm), the Passive House Institute's energy model, for three occupants. The [Future Climate](/future-climate/) page
links the weather sources. The outage study is a separate hourly [EnergyPlus](https://energyplus.net/) model on the same future
weather.

Phius comparisons use the [2024 guidebook](https://www.phius.org/phius-certification-guidebook) and
the [REVIVE 2024 standard](https://www.phius.org/phius-revive-2024-standard-document) as design
references.

### Design choices still open

- Moisture review and code approval for the batt-insulated sloped roof. Exterior roof insulation
  remains an option. Confirm the narrative's insulation minimum.
- Interior basement-wall insulation is proposed to simplify exterior finish and grade transitions;
  complete its moisture details. Confirm slab and low-slope roof products and acceptance of foam.
- Window products and quotes, with comfort and condensation checks. Keep SHGC 0.30 throughout; we
  recommend south-facing blinds and exterior shading at exposed high-sun windows.
- Humidifier equipment, with condensation checks at 35% winter RH.
- Outdoor heating and EV charging use, hot-water recirculation controls, and elevator supplier data.
- Backup-power scope: which rooms and equipment it serves.

### Engineering coordination

Final room airflows, duct sizes and insulation, ERV frost protection and fan power. Calculations at
significant thermal bridges.

### Units

Energy demand and electricity use are different quantities; each table says which it reports. Higher
R-values and lower U-values both mean less heat flow.

- 1 kWh/m²·year = approximately 0.317 kBtu/ft²·year.
- 1 W/m² = approximately 0.317 Btu/hr·ft².
- Carbon totals are metric tonnes CO₂e; one tonne equals 1,000 kg.
