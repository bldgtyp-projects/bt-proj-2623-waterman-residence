“Site Energy” is the energy purchased by the building and delivered to the site by the utility. This is the most typical energy use figure assessed when considering a site “Net-Zero” building energy balance, or the annual cost of energy for the building. The site-energy total is made up of 6 main groups: heating, cooling, hot water, lighting, electric equipment (appliances, electric vehicles, etc.), and pumps / fans.

### Phius 2024 Variant breakdown

- Heating: about **5,160 kWh/year**.
- Cooling and dehumidification: about **3,450 kWh/year**.
- Hot water: about **3,230 kWh/year**.
- Appliances, cooking, lighting and plug loads: about **6,770 kWh/year**.
- Steam humidification to **35% winter RH**: about **2,230 kWh/year**. The ERV slows winter drying
  but cannot maintain that humidity on its own, active humidifcation is included to offset the difference.
