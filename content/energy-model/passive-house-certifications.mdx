title: "Certification and verification"
---

The owners are not pursuing certification. Phius CORE 2024 is the benchmark for comparing options here.

Certification would add independent review of the model and construction, documented product
performance, field testing and moisture-safety checks. See the
[Phius certification guidebook](https://www.phius.org/phius-certification-guidebook).

These checks are worth keeping in the scope without certification:

- An early blower-door test while leaks are still accessible, and a final test against **1.0 ACH50**.
- Window comfort and condensation checks for the selected glass, frame and installation.
- Review of the roof and basement moisture details, and calculation of significant thermal bridges.
- Measured airflow in each room and a written ventilation balancing report.

The recommended option meets the four space-conditioning targets but exceeds the source energy
allowance. Its **Phius 2024** name does not imply certification or a HERS result.
