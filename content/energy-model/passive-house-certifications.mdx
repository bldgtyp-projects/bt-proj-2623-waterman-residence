title: "Certification and verification"
---

The owners are not pursuing certification. [Phius CORE 2024](https://www.phius.org/phius-certification-guidebook) is used as a reference for comparing options here.

Certification would add independent review of the model and construction, documented product
performance, field testing and moisture-safety checks. See the
[Phius certification guidebook](https://www.phius.org/phius-certification-guidebook).

Some of the Passive House checks are still worth keeping in the scope without certification. We recommend, at minimum:
- An early blower-door test while leaks are still accessible, and a final test against a target of **1.0 ACH50**.
- Window comfort and condensation checks for the final selected glass, frame and installation.
- Review of the roof and basement moisture details, and calculation of significant thermal bridges during the CD phase.
- Measured airflow in each room and a written ventilation balancing report.
