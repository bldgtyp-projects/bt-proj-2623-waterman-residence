The annual model uses projected Pittsburgh weather for **2040 to 2059** under
[SSP2-4.5](https://www.ipcc.ch/report/ar6/wg1/chapter/summary-for-policymakers/), the IPCC's intermediate emissions scenario, with an annual mean
of about **57.1°F**. [Future Climate](/future-climate/) explains how we built it.

Heating loads use historical winter conditions, so the heating equipment is sized for the cold snaps
Pittsburgh gets today.

The model places the site at **283 m** elevation. Confirm it against the final survey.

Pittsburgh is in [IECC](https://codes.iccsafe.org/content/IECC2021P2/chapter-3-ce-general-requirements) climate zone 5A, which sets the code reference.
