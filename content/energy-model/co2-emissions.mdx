title: "Operating carbon emissions"
---

Operating emissions come from the modeled electricity use at **0.416 kg CO<sub>2</sub>e/kWh**, the RFC
West grid rate in [EPA eGRID2023, Table 1](https://www.epa.gov/system/files/documents/2025-06/summary_tables_rev2.pdf).
Construction materials and equipment manufacture are not included.

| Option | Operating emissions (metric tonnes CO₂e/year) |
| --- | ---: |
| Code Minimum, code-value reference | 15.3 |
| As-Drawn | 13.6 |
| Improved Envelope | 12.2 |
| Improved Mech. | 11.8 |
| **Phius 2024** | **9.8** |

The recommended option cuts emissions **28%** from As-Drawn, matching its electricity saving. The grid
rate is held constant, so the figures don't credit a cleaner future grid.

Solar PV could cut grid purchases further. Sizing an array needs the usable roof area and its shading.
These figures include no PV or battery.
