To assess the building's performance we have tested it in five configurations (variants). Variants 2
through 5 are cumulative: each one carries forward the improvements of the one before it.

1. **Code Minimum:** A version which meets but does not exceed the **<VarLink
hrefKey="narrative.energy_code.code_base_url" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><Var
k="narrative.energy_code.name" /> / <Var k="narrative.energy_code.zone" /></VarLink>** minimums for assembly
R-values, window U-values, airtightness (<Var k="narrative.energy_code.ach_limit" /> ACH50) and equipment
efficiencies. This variant assumes a typical residential "extract-only" (bath-fan) ventilation system with
no heat recovery. Like every variant in this report, it is all-electric, with heat pumps for heating,
cooling and hot water.

2. **As-Drawn:** The house as currently designed by mossArchitects. The assemblies follow the schematic-design
narrative and drawings: 4" of continuous mineral-wool board outside insulated 2x6 walls, an insulated roof
deck with continuous insulation above the sheathing, an insulated basement, and an insulated floor over
the garage and porches. The effective assembly R-values are listed in the
[Recommended Assemblies](/building_envelope/#recommended-assemblies) section. This variant keeps code-level
airtightness (3.0 ACH50) and adds a balanced heat-recovery ventilator (HRV) at about 72% effective heat
recovery.

3. **Improved Envelope:** Tightens the air barrier to 2.0 ACH50, the architect's stated maximum, and assumes
more careful thermal-bridge detailing at the corners, eaves, balcony and garage junction.

4. **Improved Mech.:** Replaces the HRV with an energy-recovery ventilator (ERV) that also returns moisture:
about 76% effective heat recovery and 29% moisture recovery.

5. **Phius 2024 [RECOMMENDED]:** The package that brings the house to the **Phius CORE 2024** performance
targets. Airtightness improves to 1.0 ACH50, thermal bridges are designed out to a minimal allowance, the
ERV is a high-efficiency unit (about 78% heat recovery and 48% moisture recovery after duct losses), and
summer sun protection is added at the most exposed windows.

<div class="btwr-callout">
  <strong>Why these steps</strong>
  <span>
    The As-Drawn assemblies are already well insulated, so the variants that follow focus on what the
    insulation alone cannot fix: air leakage, heat lost through junctions and the ventilation air stream.
    Those are lower-cost changes than adding insulation thickness, and on this house they return more.
  </span>
</div>
