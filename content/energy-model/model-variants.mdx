We compared five options. Each step after As-Drawn keeps the improvements before it.

| Option | Airtightness (ACH50) | Modeled installed window U-value (Btu/hr·ft²·°F) | Effective heat recovery | Moisture recovery |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Code Minimum, code-value reference | 3.0 | 0.299 | Approximately zero | Zero |
| As-Drawn | 3.0 | 0.264 | 70% | Zero |
| Improved Envelope | 2.0 | 0.229 | 70% | Zero |
| Improved Mech. | 2.0 | 0.229 | 74% | 29% |
| **Phius 2024, recommended** | **1.0** | **0.199** | **76%** | **48%** |

All options use SHGC **0.30** on every facade and three occupants. Lower U-values mean less heat loss.

- **Code Minimum** uses Pennsylvania's amended 2021 whole-assembly U-values for climate zone 5: 0.051
  for framed walls, 0.026 for roofs, 0.033 for exposed floors and 0.050 for basement walls, with an
  R-10 slab. See the [adopted U-factor table](https://www.pacodeandbulletin.gov/secure/pacode/data/034/chapter403/s403.21.html).
- **As-Drawn** uses the assemblies on the [Envelope](/building_envelope/) page and an HRV.
- **Improved Envelope** adds better windows and tighter air sealing, and cuts the thermal-bridge
  allowance from 15% to 10%.
- **Improved Mech.** switches to an ERV, which adds moisture recovery.
- **Phius 2024** reaches 1.0 ACH50, better windows and a higher-efficiency ERV, with a 5% thermal-bridge
  allowance.

Wall, roof and floor insulation is the same from As-Drawn onward. Every option uses heat pumps, steam
humidification to 35% winter RH and an elevator allowance. None includes PV.
