We use the [Phius CORE 2024](https://www.phius.org/phius-certification-guidebook) space-conditioning targets to compare the different variants. While we know the home is not pursuing Passive House certification, these targets are still a useful benchmark to evaluate the modeled performance against. _Heating/Cooling-Demand_ refers the total heat added or removed over a year. _Peak-Load_ describes the rate of heat flow needed on the design day (peak outdoor conditions) to maintain comfortable conditions within the home.
| Option | Heating demand (kBtu/ft²·year) | Cooling demand, total (kBtu/ft²·year) | Peak heating (Btu/hr·ft²) | Peak cooling, sensible (Btu/hr·ft²) |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Code Minimum, reference | 15.26 | 3.49 | 12.12 | 1.88 |
| Moderate Envelope | 11.44 | 3.58 | 10.23 | 1.74 |
| As-Drawn | 10.69 | 3.67 | 9.90 | 1.75 |
| Improved Envelope | 7.92 | 3.44 | 7.32 | 1.59 |
| Improved Mech. | 7.71 | 3.27 | 7.22 | 1.58 |
| **Phius 2024** | **5.08** | **2.96** | **4.68** | **1.42** |
