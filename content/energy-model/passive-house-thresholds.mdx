We use the Phius CORE 2024 space-conditioning targets to compare the options. Annual demand is the heat
added or removed over a year. Peak load is the rate needed on the design day. Neither is electricity use.

Peak cooling counts **temperature only**. The equipment must also remove moisture
([Phius 2024 guidebook](https://www.phius.org/phius-certification-guidebook), §1.3.3.1).

| Option | Heating demand (kBtu/ft²·year) | Cooling demand, total (kBtu/ft²·year) | Peak heating (Btu/hr·ft²) | Peak cooling, sensible (Btu/hr·ft²) |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Design target | 8.60 | 5.90 | 5.90 | 2.10 |
| Code Minimum, reference | 16.22 | 3.67 | 12.56 | 2.07 |
| As-Drawn | 11.41 | 3.86 | 10.33 | 1.93 |
| Improved Envelope | 8.46 | 3.64 | 7.65 | 1.76 |
| Improved Mech. | 8.27 | 3.47 | 7.55 | 1.75 |
| **Phius 2024** | **5.50** | **3.17** | **4.94** | **1.58** |

Values are per ft² of the 4,950 ft² modeled floor area. The recommended case is below all four targets,
with a **16%** margin on peak heating. Improved Envelope meets the annual heating target but not peak
heating.

The recommended whole-house heating load is about **24,400 Btu/hour**, against **51,100** for
As-Drawn. Size equipment from room-by-room loads and manufacturer capacity at the outdoor design
temperature. Oversized equipment cycles more and controls humidity poorly.

### Other criteria

**1.0 ACH50** works out to about **0.056 CFM50/ft²** of enclosure, below the 0.060 Phius limit. The
blower-door test on the finished house confirms it.

The package exceeds the source energy allowance: about **61,400 kWh/year** against **19,650** for
three occupants, with no PV. Source energy counts the energy used to generate and deliver electricity.
