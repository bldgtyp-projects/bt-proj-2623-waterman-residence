The model is built from the mossArchitects drawings and Revit model received on
**<Var k="source_files.cad_files_received_date" />**. It includes the neighboring houses, which stand
only a few feet from each side wall, and the two large rear-yard trees. See
[Site shading](/windows/#site-shading).

The house is about 56 ft deep, 37 ft wide and 47 ft from basement floor to main ridge. It is
<Var k="narrative.user_defined.stories" /> on a <Var k="narrative.user_defined.lot_size" /> infill lot,
with a one-story garage at the rear. The garage and porches are outside the thermal envelope.

The model has <Var k="narrative.user_defined.icfa_ft2" /> ft² of floor area and
<Var k="narrative.user_defined.envelope_area_ft2" /> ft² of enclosure, roughly
**<Var k="narrative.user_defined.envelope_to_icfa_ratio" /> ft² of enclosure per ft² of floor area**.
Less enclosure per floor area means fewer surfaces losing heat.

The roof planes, dormers, bay window, balcony and garage junction add corners and edges. These junctions will 
require additional care during detailing to reduce thermal bridges and ensure air-barrier continuity; see [Envelope](/building_envelope/).
