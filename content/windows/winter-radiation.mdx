The images below compare solar exposure at each window, including shading from the neighbors, porches and trees. Colors show exposure relative to the rest of the house. How much heat a window admits also
depends on its area and SHGC.
| Window orientation  | Window area (ft²) |
| --- | ---: |
| North | 252 |
| East | 154 |
| South | 226 |
| West | 164 |

"North" is the street facade, which faces north-northeast.

The rear stair-tower and dormer glazing get the most winter sun, and the sunroom doors get a lot, though since it is outside the envelope of the home this solar gain does not entirely transmit to the interior. The
lower side windows get much less because of the close neighbors. The street-facing windows are low on
the scale but still bring in daylight.

Keep the glass that admits useful winter sun and reduce the windows which do not. Before shrinking low-exposure windows, weigh daylight, views and comfort.
