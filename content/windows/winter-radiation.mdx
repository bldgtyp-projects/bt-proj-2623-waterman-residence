These images compare solar exposure at each window, including shading from the neighbors, porches and
trees. Colors show exposure relative to the rest of the house. How much heat a window admits also
depends on its area and SHGC.

The house has about **978 ft² of windows**:

| Model orientation group | Window area, rounded (ft²) |
| --- | ---: |
| North | 252 |
| East | 271 |
| South | 272 |
| West | 184 |

"North" is the street facade, which faces north-northeast.

The rear stair-tower and dormer glazing get the most winter sun, and the sunroom doors get a lot. The
lower side windows get much less because of the close neighbors. The street-facing windows are low on
the scale but still bring in daylight.

Keep the glass that admits useful winter sun. Before shrinking low-exposure windows, weigh daylight,
views and comfort.
