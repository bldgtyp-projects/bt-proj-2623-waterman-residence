title: "Window comfort"
---

A window can meet an energy target and still feel cold to sit beside. Glass and frame temperatures,
drafts and window height all matter. Check large and tall windows before fixing the heating layout.

The recommended option uses window U-values of about **0.199**, down from **0.264** in As-Drawn, with
SHGC 0.30 throughout. Lower U-values mean less heat loss. We will review supplier quotes with the
design team.

### Choosing the windows

Aim for **whole-window U-0.15 Btu/hr·ft²·°F** when selecting products. That is better than the
recommended option, so its benefit is not in the energy results.

Check comfort pane by pane, using each window's head height and Pittsburgh's 99% winter design
temperature ([Phius 2024 window-comfort method](https://www.phius.org/phius-certification-guidebook),
Appendix N-3). Start with the tallest glazing and the main seating areas. Check frames and glass edges
for condensation at the planned indoor humidity.

Set the windows within the exterior insulation and detail the air and water seals.

Keep **SHGC 0.30 throughout** for a simpler window order. We recommend interior blinds at south-facing
windows and exterior shading at exposed high-sun openings. Decide on perimeter heating after the
window-by-window comfort checks.
