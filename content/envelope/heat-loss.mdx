title: "Heat loss by assembly"
---

The chart shows how much heat each opaque assembly type loses over the heating season as its R-value
changes. Each curve represents one assembly at its actual area, and the markers show the R-value modeled for each variant.

Note that the curves flatten as R-values rise: the same added insulation saves less in an assembly that is
already well insulated. However, area matters as much as R-value: the brick-clad wall and the sloped roof sit
highest on the chart because they are the largest surfaces on the home, while the small roof deck and exposed floor lose little heat,
even at modest R-values, since there is not much of these assemblies on the building.
