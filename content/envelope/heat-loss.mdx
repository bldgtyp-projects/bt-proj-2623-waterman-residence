title: "Heat loss by assembly"
---

The chart shows how much heat each opaque assembly loses over the heating season as its R-value
changes. Each curve represents one assembly at its actual area, and the markers show the R-value modeled in each variant.

The curves flatten as R-values rise: the same added insulation saves less in an assembly that is
already well insulated. However area matters as much as R-value: the brick-clad wall and the sloped roof sit
highest because they are the largest surfaces, while the small roof deck and exposed floor lose little heat
even at modest R-values.
