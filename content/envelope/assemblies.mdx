We propose the assemblies below as alternatives to parts of the architect's August 21, 2026
schematic-design narrative. The main departures are the sloped roof, basement wall and slab, and
foam insulation in the low-slope roof. The roof and basement-wall changes simplify construction;
their moisture details and approval remain to be resolved with the design team.

All calculated R-values include framing, brick ties and cladding fasteners.

### For Discussion

- **Sloped roof.** Keep the batt-insulated roof with an interior INTELLO PLUS or comparable smart
  membrane, ADHERO over the plywood and a ventilated cavity under the shingle or faux-slate roofing. Omitting the
  narrative's 6 inches of exterior stone wool simplifies roof attachment and edge details, but reduces
  thermal performance and changes the moisture-control strategy. Complete moisture and code review after discussion with team.
- **Basement wall.** We propose interior insulation to avoid the difficult finish and grade transitions
  over exterior foundation insulation. Complete the interior air/vapor-control and foundation moisture
  details before construction. Exterior insulation remains an alternative for discussion.
- **Low-slope roof and slab.** Both use foam, departing from the narrative's preference to minimize
  plastics. Retain XPS beneath the slab for now. The narrative's Comfortboard 110 is an alternative
  subject to the structural engineer's review and sign-off for this slab, and confirmation of site
  moisture conditions. See ROCKWOOL's
  [below-grade technical bulletin, version 1.2, March 2025](https://d-cdn.rockwool.com/syssiteassets/o2-rockwool/documentation/technical-bulletins/commercial/below-grade-technical-bulletin-v1.1.pdf).

The architect coordinates final layers, structural supports and drainage.
