Below are the building envelope assemblies as modeled, with our recommendations. They follow the mossArchitects
schematic-design narrative and drawings, and should be reviewed by the design team to confirm they suit the project.

All R-values quoted here are **effective whole-assembly** values, calculated in two dimensions with
[Flixo](https://www.flixo.com/). They account for the framing, the brick ties and cladding fasteners, and the other
thermal bridges inside each assembly. They run lower than the nominal R-value of the insulation alone, and they are
the numbers that drive the energy results.

| Assembly | Area (ft²) | As modeled, all variants | IECC 2021 CZ5 (U-factor equivalent) |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| Brick-clad wall | 2,613 | R-29.9 | R-22.2 |
| Wood-clad wall | 1,911 | R-32.9 | R-22.2 |
| Sloped roof | 2,338 | R-32.7 | R-41.7 |
| Roof deck (low-slope) | 164 | R-55.9 | R-41.7 |
| Exposed floor | 249 | R-33.3 | R-30.3 |
| Basement wall | 1,967 | R-22.5 | R-20.0 |
| Basement slab | 1,862 | R-21.1 | Not required |

The assemblies are the same in every variant from As-Drawn onward. The variants instead change airtightness,
thermal bridges at junctions and the ventilation system, as described in the
[Model Variants](/energy_model/#model-variants) section.

  <strong>Foam-free goal</strong>
  <span>
    The architect's narrative sets out foam-free, vapor-open assemblies. Two of the assemblies as modeled still
    use foam: polyisocyanurate above the low-slope roof deck and XPS below the basement slab. Mineral-wool or
    cellular-glass alternatives exist for both and are noted on each card below. The R-values will change slightly
    with the substitution.
