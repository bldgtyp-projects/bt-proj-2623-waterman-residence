As modeled: **R-22.5**, U-0.044 Btu/hr·ft²·°F.

We propose a concrete/CMU foundation wall with an interior 2x6 stud wall filled with mineral-wool
batts, departing from the narrative's masonry wall with 6 inches of _exterior_ stone wool. Note that the diagram
omits the interior smart membrane described below. Interior insulation simplifies exterior finishes and waterproofing but does require more careful interior-side air and moisture control.

Interior insulation avoids the difficult cladding and grade transitions over exterior insulation on
this site. However, exterior insulation would keep the foundation warmer and remains an alternative if the
team resolves those finishes. The proposed interior approach requires a separate moisture review;
it retains the need for exterior waterproofing and drainage.

**Batts alone cannot control air or moisture at the cold foundation.**
Add a continuous [INTELLO PLUS](https://proclima.com/products/internal-sealing/intello-plus/planning-notes)
or comparable smart membrane, sealed at every joint and connection. Confirm the complete assembly's
moisture performance before construction. The wall still
needs capillary separation, waterproofing, drainage and a drying path. See the DOE's
[basement-insulation guidance](https://basc.pnnl.gov/resource-guides/air-sealed-insulated-basements).

Carry the insulation and air barrier through the partly exposed street-side wall and up into the
above-grade wall.
