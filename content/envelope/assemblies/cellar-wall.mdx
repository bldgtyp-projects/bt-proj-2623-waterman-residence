As modeled: **R-22.5** (U-0.044 Btu/hr-ft<sup>2</sup>-F).

- 8" concrete foundation wall with an interior insulated stud wall: 2x6 studs with 5½" of
  [Rockwool Comfortbatt](https://www.rockwool.com/north-america/products/comfortbatt/) and gypsum board.
- The schematic-design narrative placed 6" of stone wool outside the foundation, below a drainage mat (R-24
  nominal). If that option is still open, it has advantages: it keeps the concrete warm and inside the thermal
  envelope, removes the risk of condensation behind the interior framing, and leaves the interior free for finishes
  and services. Worth confirming with the design team which approach is intended.
- If the insulation stays on the interior, use only vapor-open, moisture-tolerant materials against the concrete
  and confirm the exterior waterproofing and drainage are continuous.
- The basement is partly exposed on the street side, where grade falls toward McPherson Boulevard. Carry the wall
  insulation continuously up to the above-grade wall insulation so there is no gap at the transition.
