As modeled: **R-22.5**, U-0.044 Btu/hr·ft²·°F.

An 8-inch concrete foundation with an interior 2x6 stud wall filled with mineral-wool batts. The
narrative calls for CMU with 6 inches of exterior stone wool and exterior waterproofing. The diagram
omits the interior smart membrane described below; the construction detail needs it.

**We prefer exterior insulation.** It keeps the foundation warm and avoids interior condensation.
Finishing the exposed insulation is hard on this site, so review the cladding and grade transitions with
the architect first. Use a below-grade-rated product with continuous waterproofing and drainage.

**If the insulation stays inside, batts alone are not safe.** Indoor air would reach the cold foundation.
Add a continuous [INTELLO PLUS](https://proclima.com/products/internal-sealing/intello-plus/planning-notes)
or comparable smart membrane, sealed at every joint and connection. The wall still needs capillary
separation, waterproofing, drainage and a drying path. See the DOE's
[basement-insulation guidance](https://basc.pnnl.gov/resource-guides/air-sealed-insulated-basements).

Carry the insulation and air barrier through the partly exposed street-side wall and up into the
above-grade wall.
