As modeled: **R-33.3**, U-0.030 Btu/hr·ft²·°F.

Wood flooring and plywood over joists filled with 11¼ inches of mineral-wool batts, about 249 ft² in
total. Confirm where it applies: garage, porch or bay window.

Connect the floor's air barrier to the adjoining walls. Keep the batts tight to it and shield their
underside from air movement.

Over the garage, combine the air sealing with the required fire separation, and seal every penetration.
