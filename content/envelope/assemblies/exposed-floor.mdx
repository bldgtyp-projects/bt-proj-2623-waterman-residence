As drawn and modeled: **R-33.3** (U-0.030 Btu/hr-ft<sup>2</sup>-F).

{/* TODO(ed): confirm where this assembly occurs (garage? porch? bay?). */}

- Floors with outdoor or unconditioned air below: wood flooring on plywood subfloor over joists filled with 11¼" of
  [Rockwool Comfortbatt](https://www.rockwool.com/north-america/products/comfortbatt/).
- Provide a continuous air barrier at the underside (taped sheathing or membrane), sealed to the wall air barrier
  at every edge. Floors over garages and porches are a common path for both heat loss and garage air.
- Where any such floor is over the garage, the air barrier also protects indoor air quality. Treat it as a gas-tight
  separation, as the code requires.
