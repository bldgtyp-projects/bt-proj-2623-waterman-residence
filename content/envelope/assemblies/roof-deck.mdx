As drawn and modeled: **R-55.9** (U-0.018 Btu/hr-ft<sup>2</sup>-F).

- The low-slope membrane roofs, including the elevator overrun: membrane over cover board, 4" of polyiso above
  the deck, plywood sheathing, and 11¼" of [Rockwool Comfortbatt](https://www.rockwool.com/north-america/products/comfortbatt/)
  between the joists.
- The polyiso is the one foam layer above grade. If the foam-free goal applies here, stone-wool roof board
  (about R-4 per inch against polyiso's R-5.7) needs roughly 6" for the same result.
- Mechanical fasteners through the above-deck insulation are included in the R-value. Adhered insulation
  avoids them.
- Detail the transition where these roofs meet the sloped roofs and walls with a continuous air barrier; this is
  one of the junctions identified in the [Building Airtightness](/building_envelope/#building-airtightness) section.
