As modeled: **R-55.9**, U-0.018 Btu/hr·ft²·°F.

A membrane and cover board over 4 inches of polyiso, plywood, and 11¼ inches of mineral-wool batts
between joists.

The polyiso conflicts with the narrative's foam-free goal. A mineral-wool roof board can replace it;
confirm thickness, structural loads and the membrane manufacturer's requirements, and we will
recalculate.

Check fastener heat loss, wind uplift and condensation control for this unvented roof. Detail continuous
air and water control where it meets the walls, the pitched roofs and the elevator overrun.
