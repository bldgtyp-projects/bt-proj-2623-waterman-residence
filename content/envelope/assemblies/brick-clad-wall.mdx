As modeled: **R-29.9**, U-0.033 Btu/hr·ft²·°F.

Anchored brick veneer and a drained cavity, 4 inches of
[Comfortboard](https://www.rockwool.com/north-america/products/comfortboard/), plywood and an
air-control membrane, with mineral-wool batts between 2x6 studs.

Brick ties cross the exterior insulation and represent thermal bridges. Stainless ties lose less heat than carbon steel. Confirm type and spacing with the structural engineer.

Where shelf angles are needed, use stand-off brackets ([Ferro Fast](https://ferocorp.com/products/fast-thermal-brackets/) or sim) that keep most of the angle outside the insulation,
and detail their air and water seals.

The narrative's preferred exterior insulation is wood fiber, nominal R-14 for 4 inches against R-16 for stone wool. We will recalculate if wood fiber is chosen.
