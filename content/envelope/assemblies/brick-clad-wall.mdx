As modeled: **R-29.9**, U-0.033 Btu/hr·ft²·°F.

Anchored brick veneer and a drained cavity, 4 inches of
[Comfortboard](https://www.rockwool.com/north-america/products/comfortboard/), plywood and an
air-control membrane, with mineral-wool batts between 2x6 studs.

Brick ties cross the exterior insulation. Stainless ties lose less heat than carbon steel; the structural
engineer sets type and spacing, and the fewer the better.

Where shelf angles are needed, use stand-off brackets that keep most of the angle outside the insulation,
and detail their air and water seals.

The narrative's preferred exterior insulation is wood fiber, nominal R-14 for 4 inches against R-16 for
stone wool. We will recalculate if wood fiber is chosen.
