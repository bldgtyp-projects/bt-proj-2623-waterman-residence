As drawn and modeled: **R-29.9** (U-0.033 Btu/hr-ft<sup>2</sup>-F).

- Anchored brick veneer with an air cavity, 4" of
  [Rockwool Comfortboard](https://www.rockwool.com/north-america/products/comfortboard/), plywood sheathing with a
  vapor-open air-control membrane, and 2x6 studs with 5½" of
  [Rockwool Comfortbatt](https://www.rockwool.com/north-america/products/comfortbatt/).
- The stainless-steel brick ties through the 4" board, at 16" o.c., are included in the R-value, and they are the
  main reason this wall runs about R-3 below the wood-clad wall. Use a low-conductivity tie (stainless steel or a thermally broken
  tie) and keep tie spacing at the minimum the structural design allows.
- Avoid steel shelf angles. If the brick has to be supported above the foundation, use stand-off brackets so the
  angle sits outside the insulation, and let us model the detail.
- The architect's narrative lists a 4" wood-fiber board (R-14 nominal) as the primary option, against R-16 nominal
  for the stone-wool board modeled here. If wood fiber is selected we will recalculate the assembly with it.
