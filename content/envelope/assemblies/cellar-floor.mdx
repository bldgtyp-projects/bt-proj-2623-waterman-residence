As modeled: **R-21.1**, U-0.047 Btu/hr·ft²·°F.

A 4-inch concrete slab over 4 inches of XPS. The narrative calls for 3 inches of stone wool, nominal
R-12. Confirm which the team intends.

A foam-free product must carry the slab loads over the long term. Stone wool, cellular glass and
foamed-glass aggregate perform differently at the same thickness, so choose one with the structural
engineer.

Connect the slab-edge insulation to the wall insulation. Lay a sealed sub-slab vapor barrier, 15 mil per
the narrative, over the capillary break and tie it into the wall's air and moisture control.

Include a radon-resistant foundation and test after occupancy. See
[EPA guidance](https://www.epa.gov/radon/radon-resistant-construction-basics-and-techniques) for the
soil-gas layer, membrane and vent stack.
