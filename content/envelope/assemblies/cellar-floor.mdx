As modeled: **R-21.1** (U-0.047 Btu/hr-ft<sup>2</sup>-F).

- 4" concrete slab over 4" of XPS foam, on a vapor barrier and capillary break.
- The architect's narrative calls for 3" of stone-wool board (R-12 nominal) in place of the foam. If the foam-free
  goal applies here, sub-slab rated stone wool or foamed-glass aggregate are the alternatives; match the R-value
  modeled here with about 5" of stone wool.
- Carry the insulation continuously to the basement wall insulation at the slab edge. This junction is the
  most common gap in a basement envelope.
- Provide a continuous, sealed vapor barrier (the narrative specifies 15 mil) below the slab, lapped and sealed to the
  wall waterproofing. It is also the radon barrier: provide a passive radon vent stack, which can be activated later
  if testing shows it is needed.
