As modeled: **R-32.7** (U-0.031 Btu/hr-ft<sup>2</sup>-F). Suggested: **R-50 or better**.

- The main pitched roofs and the third-floor cathedral ceilings: roofing over battens, plywood sheathing, and
  11¼" of [Rockwool Comfortbatt](https://www.rockwool.com/north-america/products/comfortbatt/) between the rafters.
- At 2,338 ft² this is one of the largest surfaces on the house, and as modeled it is the weakest above-grade
  assembly, below the code's U-factor equivalent of about R-42.
- The schematic-design narrative showed 6" of stone-wool board above the deck, under a vented batten cavity, which
  would bring the assembly above R-50. With at least half of the R-value above the deck, an unvented roof also stays
  dry without relying on a vapor retarder. If that layer has been removed from the design, we suggest the team
  reconsider it; if it is still intended, the model will be updated to include it.
- Unvented roof assemblies must follow the code provisions in
  [IRC Section R806.5](https://codes.iccsafe.org/content/IRC2021P3/chapter-8-roof-ceiling-construction).
