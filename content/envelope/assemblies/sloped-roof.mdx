As modeled: **R-32.7**, U-0.031 Btu/hr·ft²·°F.

In order to simplify construction, we propose a departure from the SD narrative: 11¼ inches of mineral-wool batts between rafters,
with no continuous exterior insulation.
This simplifies roof attachment and eave, rake and dormer details compared with the narrative's
6 inches of exterior stone wool. Use vapor-permeable
[ADHERO underlay](https://proclima.com/products/external-sealing/solitex-adhero-3000/planning-notes)
over the plywood and a continuous ventilated cavity under the roofing. The drawings show asphalt
shingle or faux slate; the roofing material does not change the energy results. The diagram omits
both membranes; the construction detail needs them.

Inside, use a continuous [INTELLO PLUS](https://proclima.com/products/internal-sealing/intello-plus/planning-notes)
or comparable smart vapor retarder. Tape the joints and seal penetrations and edges.

The tradeoff is lower thermal performance and colder structural sheathing. The narrative calls for
nominal R-52 to R-54 and at least half the insulation R-value above the deck. Our proposed roof
changes that moisture-control strategy and adds an interior smart vapor retarder.

### Design-team coordination

- Confirm moisture performance with the membrane supplier, using the plywood, roof exposure and
  **35% winter indoor RH**.
- Detail the ventilated cavity's inlets and outlets.
- The rafter cavity itself is unvented. Settle the code approval path under
  [IRC R806.5](https://codes.iccsafe.org/content/IRC2021P3/chapter-8-roof-ceiling-construction).
- If unvented sheathing is a concern, include typical sheathing vents along the underside of the plywood.
