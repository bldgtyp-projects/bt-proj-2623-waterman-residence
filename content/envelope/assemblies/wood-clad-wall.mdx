As drawn and modeled: **R-32.9** (U-0.030 Btu/hr-ft<sup>2</sup>-F).

- Cladding on a vented rainscreen, 4" of
  [Rockwool Comfortboard 80](https://www.rockwool.com/north-america/products/comfortboard/) fastened with
  stainless-steel screws, plywood sheathing with a vapor-open air-control membrane, and 2x6 studs with 5½" of
  [Rockwool Comfortbatt](https://www.rockwool.com/north-america/products/comfortbatt/).
- With most of the insulation outboard of the sheathing, the sheathing stays warm in winter and the wall can dry
  to both sides. Keep every layer outboard of the sheathing vapor-open.
- Fasten the rainscreen furring through the board with long screws, not clips with metal brackets, to keep the
  fastener thermal bridges small.
