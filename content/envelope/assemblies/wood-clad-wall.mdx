As modeled: **R-32.9**, U-0.030 Btu/hr·ft²·°F.

Cladding on a ventilated rainscreen, 4 inches of
[Comfortboard](https://www.rockwool.com/north-america/products/comfortboard/), plywood and an
air-control membrane, with mineral-wool batts between 2x6 studs. We will recalculate if the team chooses
the narrative's wood-fiber option.

Exterior insulation keeps the sheathing warm. Here it is under half the wall's insulation, so select a WRB membrane's that allows for outward drying. 

Design the furring and fasteners to carry the cladding and wind loads through 4 inches of insulation,
with as little fastener heat loss as the structure allows.
