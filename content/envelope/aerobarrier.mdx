title: "AeroBarrier"
---

Hitting Passive House levels of airtightness in existing buildings is hard. The dedicated air barrier layers described above will give durable airtightness, and they should be the primary strategy.

As a backup option if the team cannot achieve the target level of airsealing, [AeroBarrier](https://aeroseal.com/industries/residential-new-construction/) is an optional aerosol sealant which can help acheive better airtightness. Note that while it can be effective, it closes small leaks only and does not replace flashing or the repair of large gaps.

If the team wants the option, bring in an installer early to review scope, sequence, pricing and
compatibility with the project's material goals. Let the first blower-door test decide whether it's
needed.
