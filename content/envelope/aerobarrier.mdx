title: "AeroBarrier"
---

A continuous, inspectable air barrier is the main air-sealing strategy. Draw and review its connections
at the dormers, balcony, bay window and garage before construction.

[AeroBarrier](https://aeroseal.com/industries/residential-new-construction/) is an optional aerosol
sealant sprayed while the house is pressurized. It closes small leaks. It does not replace flashing or
the repair of large gaps.

If the team wants the option, bring in an installer early to review scope, sequence, pricing and
compatibility with the project's material goals. Let the first blower-door test decide whether it's
needed.
