The dedicated air-barrier layer described above should deliver excellent, durable airtightness on its own, and that
is the goal. A designed, inspectable air barrier is always the primary strategy.

This house has many transitions where the air barrier depends on field workmanship: roof-to-wall junctions at
several roof planes, dormers, the bay window, the balcony, the elevator shaft and the garage connection. Where
air-sealing results are uncertain, we recommend having a backup plan in place.

[AeroBarrier](https://aeroseal.com/industries/residential-new-construction/) provides whole-building air sealing using
an aerosolized polymer that "finds" leaks and seals them. We recommend consulting an AeroBarrier installer early so that
the service can be included in the project schedule should it be needed. It costs less and disrupts less when scheduled
in advance at the right point in the sequence, after the air barrier is complete and before finishes go on, than when it
is called in to rescue a failed blower-door test.
