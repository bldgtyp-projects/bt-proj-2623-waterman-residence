title: "Air sealing"
---

We recommend **1.0 ACH50 or better** as a good target for this home. ACH50 is the air leakage
measured by a blower-door test at 50 pascals; it measures how well the house is
sealed. Good air sealing cuts drafts and heat loss, and keeps moist indoor air out of cold walls
and roofs.

Going from the code-minimum's 3.0 ACH50 to 1.0 cuts measured leakage by two-thirds. However total heating use falls by much less, because heat also leaves through walls and windows.

The air barrier is the membrane near the plywood sheathing. Show it continuous through every wall and
roof detail. The hard spots on this house are the dormers, bay window, and balcony. Tie the interior smart membranes at the roof, and at any interior-insulated basement wall, into the wall air barrier.

Write the target and the testing into the construction documents. Test once the air barrier is complete
but still accessible, fix the leaks, and test again at completion.
