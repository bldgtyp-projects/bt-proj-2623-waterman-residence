title: "Air sealing"
---

We recommend **1.0 ACH50 or better** as a good target for this home. ACH50 is the air leakage (air-changes-per-hour)
measured by a blower-door test at 50 pascals; it measures how well the house is
sealed. Good air sealing cuts drafts and heat loss, and helps keep moist indoor air out of cold walls and roofs in winter.

Going from the code-minimum's 3.0-ACH50 down to 1.0-ACH@50 cuts measured leakage by two-thirds.

The primary air barrier on this home should be the WRB membrane over the plywood sheathing. Show it continuous through every wall and
roof detail. The hard spots on this house are the dormers, bay window, and roof-deck. Tie the interior smart membranes at the roof, and at any interior-insulated basement wall, into the wall air barrier.

Write the target and the testing into the construction documents. Test once the air barrier is complete
but still accessible, fix the leaks, and test again at completion.
