title: "Outage assessment"
---

We modeled a seven-day winter outage, **February 3 to 9**, and a seven-day summer outage, **July 13 to
19**, in the future climate. Outdoor extremes reach about **−6.2°F** in winter and **100°F** in summer.
The house has three occupants. Results are averaged by floor, so individual rooms can run hotter or
colder.

### What runs during the outage

Heating, cooling, humidification and dehumidification are off. Backup power keeps the ERV running at
**15 CFM**, 5 CFM per person, per the
[REVIVE 2024 protocol](https://www.phius.org/phius-revive-2024-standard-document). Windows stay closed
in winter and open on temperature in summer.

### Results

- **Winter** meets both REVIVE limits: no floor falls below 36°F, and every floor stays within the
  comfort-shortfall limit. Indoor air still falls to about **39°F**.
- **Summer** never passes the 103°F [Heat Index](https://www.weather.gov/safety/heat-index) danger line, peaking near **100°F**. But **every floor
  exceeds the daily heat-and-humidity limit on three days**, so summer fails one of the two criteria.

### How to use these results

These results are preliminary. The outage model doesn't yet match the final assemblies and windows.
Use it to decide which rooms and systems backup power should serve, not to size a battery or generator.
A refuge room would need its own study.
