The winter assessment uses **Standard Effective Temperature (SET)**, defined in
[ASHRAE Standard 55](https://www.ashrae.org/technical-resources/bookstore/standard-55-thermal-environmental-conditions-for-human-occupancy) to evaluate how warm a room feels once surface temperatures, humidity, air movement and clothing are all considered.
[Phius-REVIVE](https://www.phius.org/phius-revive-2024-standard-document) sets a life-safety line at 36°F SET and a comfort allowance of no more than **216 °F-hours** total below 54°F SET over the assessment peroiod.

The key issue in winter is the limited solar gain. While the high performance envelope does work to retard heat loss, due to the very low solar heat gain available on the site the home sees very little meaningful solar gain to help offset this loss. As a result, over time, the home continues to cool down.

#### Winter Results

The upper floors lose five to six degrees a day and reach **19 to 27°F air temperature** on the
last morning, with SET near **32 to 39°F**. The ground holds the basement at **36°F air** and
**46°F SET**. Daytime sun lifts the upper floors a few degrees each afternoon. The night takes it
back. Indoor humidity falls to about **10% to 40%**.

 #### Improving Performance

- **Increase insulation levels:** more insulative assemblies help reduce heat loss during the winter period.
- **Increase solar gain:** while this would help to warm the home during the day, there is little practical way to do this given the current design and site shading. 
- **Backup heat:** No floor can achieve the Phius-REVIVE targets without some amount of additional heating. The basement is the closest thanks to the ground-tempering: it stays above the life-safety threshold (36°F) for the entire week.
- **Freeze protection:** With upstairs air in the 20s for two days or more, pipes anywhere above
  the basement would likely freeze. Keep plumbing runs in the basement and interior walls wherever possible to reduce this risk, and plan for draining the system or keeping a heat source on the plumbing in a long outage.
