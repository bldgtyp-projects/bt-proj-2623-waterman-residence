For the winter outage, **Phius REVIVE** uses the
[Standard Effective Temperature (SET)](https://bigladdersoftware.com/epx/docs/9-5/engineering-reference/resilience-metrics.html#set-hour),
which combines air temperature, surface temperatures, humidity and clothing into a single measure of how warm a person
feels. To comply, every zone must show:

- 0 hours below 36°F [2°C] **SET**
- Fewer than 216 °F-hours below 54°F [12°C] **SET**

The insulation, airtightness and window quality carry the house through a winter outage. The basement, surrounded by
ground that stays near 50°F, should hold its temperature best; the rooms with the most glass and roof area will cool
fastest.
