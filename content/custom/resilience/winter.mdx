Winter results use two measures: the lowest **air temperature**, and the accumulated shortfall in
**Standard Effective Temperature (SET)**. SET is how warm the room feels, accounting for surface
temperatures, humidity, air movement and clothing.

### Result

No floor falls below the **36°F air-temperature limit**. The SET shortfall below 54°F stays within the
**216 °F-hour** limit on every floor
([REVIVE 2024 §6.3.2](https://www.phius.org/phius-revive-2024-standard-document)):

| Floor | SET shortfall (°F-hours) |
| --- | ---: |
| Basement | 147 |
| First | 184 |
| Second | 188 |
| Third | 192 |

One hour at SET 52°F adds 2 °F-hours.

### Indoor conditions

Passing the limits still means cold rooms. Indoor air falls to about **39°F** and SET to about
**49°F**. Humidity drops to about **10% to 18%**.

The third floor uses **89%** of its allowance, a thin margin. Confirm it with the final envelope before
deciding on backup heat. A floor average also can't show whether every pipe and small room stays above
freezing.
