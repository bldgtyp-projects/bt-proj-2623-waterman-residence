**Phius REVIVE** uses the
[Heat Index](https://bigladdersoftware.com/epx/docs/22-2/engineering-reference/resilience-metrics.html#heat-index), a
measure of how hot it feels when humidity is combined with air temperature, to judge whether each zone stays habitable
during the summer outage. Minimum compliance requires:

- 0 hours of **Heat Index** in the "Extreme Danger" range (above 125°F [52°C]).

Humidity is the concern in Pittsburgh's summers, more than temperature alone. Without power, the house cannot
dehumidify, and the Heat Index can climb even where the air temperature stays moderate. The upper floors, which
see the most summer sun and collect the warm air rising through the house, are expected to be the warmest zones.
