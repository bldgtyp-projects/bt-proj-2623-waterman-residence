[Heat Index](https://www.weather.gov/safety/heat-index) combines temperature and humidity to measure heat stress. [REVIVE](https://www.phius.org/phius-revive-2024-standard-document) also checks average daily
temperature and humidity against a separate **deadly-day** limit. A house has to pass both.

### Result

Heat Index never exceeds **103°F**; it peaks at about **100°F**. But **every floor exceeds the daily
limit on three days**, July 17 to 19, the last three days of the outage
([REVIVE 2024 §6.4.2](https://www.phius.org/phius-revive-2024-standard-document)). Indoor air reaches
about **88°F**, with humidity between about **50% and 86%**.

### What to test next

- Exterior shading at the exposed openings, with the final envelope and windows.
- Backup cooling for the rooms the owners want to keep usable. A stand-alone dehumidifier adds heat,
  so it is no substitute.
- Battery capacity for the chosen loads and duration, including the ERV and controls.
