[Heat Index](https://www.weather.gov/safety/heat-index) combines temperature and humidity into
one felt temperature. [REVIVE](https://www.phius.org/phius-revive-2024-standard-document) also
checks each day's mean temperature and humidity against a separate **deadly-day** line.

  </span>

#### Summer Results:

- The **basement** never climbs past an **81° heat-index**.
- The **first floor heat-index** peaks at **100°F**, just under the Danger line.
- The **second and third floor heat-index** reach **106°F and 108°F** and sit above the line for **14 and 19 hours**.
    
The upper floors swing about eight degrees between night and afternoon. The windows open
whenever the house is warmer than outside, which flushes the house each night, but from July 16
the nights stay too warm to bring the zone temperatures back down efectively. The hottest afternoon, July 16, pushes the second and third floors into the mid-90s. The second floor zone has the most glass and the
third sits under the roof, so those two run hottest.

The basement stays near **69 to 79°F** primarily due to the ground-tempering. Its humidity
sits between **66% and 99%** all week however. It is a cool space, but with more damp air. A stand-alone
dehumidifier running off battery backup would likely help improve this zone and increase its resiliency, though standone dehumidifiers also add heat to the space.

#### Improving Performance

- Consider backup cooling for the rooms the owners want to keep usable upstairs, and the battery or generator capacit  to run it along with the ERV and controls for a week. 
- Level-2 Primary-bedroom and primary-closet south-west windows should have horizontal exterior shading adding in order to help reduce the heat gain during the day.
- Kitchen and Living room south-west windows would also benefit from horizontal exterior shading, though these windows are already more shaded than Level-2 thanks to the neighboring building.
- South windows in Katy's Studio would benefit from external horizontal overhang shading as well. While the tree cover will likely provide some shading benefit, this may be irregular and fixed shading may provide a more consistent benefit.
