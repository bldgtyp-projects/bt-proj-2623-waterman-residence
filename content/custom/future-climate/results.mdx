Results for the recommended option in the 2040 to 2059 climate:

| Result | Phius 2024 option | Design target |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Annual heating demand | 5.50 kBtu/ft²·year | 8.60 |
| Annual cooling demand, including moisture removal | 3.17 kBtu/ft²·year | 5.90 |
| Peak heating | 4.94 Btu/hr·ft², about 24,400 Btu/hour | 5.90 |
| Peak sensible cooling | 1.58 Btu/hr·ft², about 7,830 Btu/hour | 2.10 |
| Peak latent cooling input | About 6,630 Btu/hour | Separate from sensible target |
| Site electricity | 23,610 kWh/year | No target used here |

The recommended option meets all four space-conditioning targets in the future climate. It exceeds the
source energy allowance, so it does not meet the full Phius standard.

Moisture removal is a large part of the cooling load. The mechanical design must handle it along with
the sensible load, including when the house needs little cooling.

The report has no present-day run to compare, so these figures don't isolate the effect of climate
change.
