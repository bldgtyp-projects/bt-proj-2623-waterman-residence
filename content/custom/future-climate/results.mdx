All results in the [Energy Model](/energy_model/) section are calculated in the mid-century climate. For the
recommended **Phius 2024** package they are:

| Result, Phius 2024 package | Mid-century (SSP2-4.5, 2040 to 2059) | Phius CORE 2024 target |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Annual heating demand | 7.2 kBtu/ft²·yr | 8.6 |
| Annual cooling and dehumidification demand | 3.5 kBtu/ft²·yr | 5.9 |
| Peak heating load | 5.85 Btu/hr·ft² (about 29,000 Btu/hr) | 5.9 |
| Peak cooling load | 1.9 Btu/hr·ft² (about 15,900 Btu/hr, sensible plus latent) | 2.1 |
| Site energy | 17,340 kWh/yr | |

Three things stand out in the warmer climate:

- **Moisture is the summer load.** Of the peak cooling load, about 42% is latent (moisture) rather than sensible
  (temperature). The heat pumps should be selected for their dehumidification capacity, and the ERV's moisture recovery
  matters as much as its heat recovery.
- **Cooling and dehumidification use about half as much energy as heating** over the year, so the cooling
  equipment and its controls deserve as much design attention as the heating.
- **Peak heating does not shrink.** The heating design conditions are held at their historical values, so the heat
  pumps still have to be sized for a Pittsburgh cold snap even as the average winter gets milder.
