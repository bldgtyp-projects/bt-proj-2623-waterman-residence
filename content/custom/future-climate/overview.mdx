title: "Why use a future climate?"
dek: "Why this house is assessed against mid-century weather as well as today's."
---

A house built now will run through a changing climate. At the owners' request, the annual energy model
uses **SSP2-4.5 for 2040 to 2059**: projected mid-century weather, not a forecast for any one year.

SSP2-4.5 is an intermediate emissions scenario, neither a worst case nor a forecast. Adding other
scenarios would show how the design holds up under stronger warming.

The projected monthly changes come from the
[ORNL Cross-Model future weather dataset](https://zenodo.org/records/10719179), checked against
[NOAA county projections](https://cris.climate.gov/datasets/cris-zonal-statistics-by-county-1).
They add about **2.8°F** of annual warming to a recent typical year, for an annual mean of about
**57.1°F** at the site.

The outage study uses the same future weather, with the REVIVE heat-wave and cold-snap extremes
described under [How the weather is used](#future-climate-assessment).
