### Hourly weather

We started from the
[Climate.OneBuilding Pittsburgh International TMYx 2011 to 2025](https://climate.onebuilding.org/WMO_Region_4_North_and_Central_America/USA_United_States_of_America/PA_Pennsylvania/USA_PA_Pittsburgh.Intl.AP.725200_TMYx.2011-2025.zip)
file, a typical year assembled from recent months.

We took the monthly temperature and dew-point changes between the ORNL dataset's 2000 to 2019 and
2040 to 2059 periods and applied them to the hourly file along a smooth seasonal curve. Humidity and
sky radiation follow from the new temperature and dew point.

The method keeps the original sequence of weather, sun, cloud and wind. It does not project changes in
storms, wind, sunshine or heat-wave length.

### Monthly PHPP climate

The monthly climate for the annual [PHPP](https://passivehouse.com/04_phpp/04_phpp.htm) model gets the same seasonal changes. Its annual mean is about
**57.1°F** at the site elevation.
