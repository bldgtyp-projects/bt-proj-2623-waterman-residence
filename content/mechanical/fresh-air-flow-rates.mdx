title: "Room airflows"
---

The ERV is sized at about **225 CFM at high speed** and **173 CFM in normal operation**, balanced
between supply and extract. The ventilation designer sets the final room airflows.

Supply goes to bedrooms and living spaces, including the basement. Extract comes from the kitchen,
bathrooms and laundry rooms. Halls, stairs and door gaps carry air between them.

**The third-floor studio is shown as an extract room at about 25 CFM.** A regularly occupied studio
should get supply air, with the extract moved to wet rooms. We recommend changing it.

Size the system for guests as well as the three regular occupants.

The **624 CFM** range hood is a separate system; see [Kitchen exhaust](#appliances-and-venting).
