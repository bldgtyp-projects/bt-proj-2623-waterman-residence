title: "Room airflows"
---

The mechanical engineer should size airflow to code and room needs, no higher. Extra airflow dries the house in winter and adds excess moisture in summer.

The airflows on the plans are our proposed scheme and the basis of the energy model, but should be updated once the mechanical engineer or installer has reviewed and sized the equipment. The ERV is
sized at about **225 CFM at high speed** and **173 CFM in normal operation**, balanced between supply
and extract.

Supply air goes to bedrooms, offices and living spaces, including the basement. Extract comes from the
kitchen, bathrooms and laundry rooms. Halls, stairs and door gaps carry transfer air between these zones.

The **624 CFM** range hood is a separate system from the ERV; see [Kitchen exhaust](#appliances-and-venting).
