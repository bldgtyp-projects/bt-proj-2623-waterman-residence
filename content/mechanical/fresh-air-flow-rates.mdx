The fresh-air system should be sized around the room-by-room supply, transfer and extract strategy rather than around a
single whole-building ventilation rate. The table below shows the modeled airflows for design coordination and
commissioning.

**<Var k="narrative.user_defined.standard_airflow_cfm" /> CFM** at its normal operating (medium) speed, balanced supply
to extract. Supply goes to the living room, library and guest room, sunroom, primary bedroom, office, snug, the
basement and the first-floor hall. Extract is drawn from the kitchen, all bathrooms and both laundry rooms. Stairs,
halls, closets and the elevator are transfer zones with no dedicated supply or extract.
