Choose the enthalpy-exchanger version (ERV) for moisture recovery. Once final equipment is selected, we will update recovery and fan power to the actual airflow and duct pressure; the headline rating may not apply depending.

The recommended case reaches about **76% heat recovery** and **48% moisture recovery**.

Put the ERV inside the thermal envelope, with access to filters, core and drains. Keep the
outdoor ducts short, insulated and airtight, with a continuous vapor jacket. 

### Commissioning

After installation of the system, measure every grille with the filters in, adjust, and issue a balancing report at normal and boost speeds. Total supply and extract should agree within **10%**, and every room should get its scheduled airflow.

Confirm frost protection for Pittsburgh winters and access for filter changes. We recommend [MERV 13](https://www.epa.gov/indoor-air-quality-iaq/what-merv-rating) or
better on the outdoor air if the unit can run it at design airflow.
