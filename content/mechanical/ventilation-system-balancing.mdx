The architect proposes a
<VarLink hrefKey="narrative.mechanical.erv.link"><Var k="narrative.mechanical.erv.type_name" /></VarLink>.
Choose the enthalpy-exchanger version for moisture recovery. Confirm recovery and fan power at the
actual airflow and duct pressure; the headline rating may not apply.

The recommended case reaches about **76% heat recovery** and **48% moisture recovery**. Keep the
outdoor ducts short, insulated and airtight, with a continuous vapor jacket. The ventilation designer
sets final routes and sizes.

Put the ERV inside the thermal envelope, with access to filters, core and drains. Compare the basement
and upper-floor locations by duct runs and service access.

### Commissioning

The ventilation designer sets the room airflows. After installation, measure every grille with the
filters in, adjust, and issue a balancing report at normal and boost speeds. Total supply and extract
should agree within **10%**, and every room should get its scheduled airflow.

Confirm frost protection for Pittsburgh winters and access for filter changes. We recommend [MERV 13](https://www.epa.gov/indoor-air-quality-iaq/what-merv-rating) or
better on the outdoor air if the unit can run it at design airflow.
