title: "Ventilation design checks"
---

These checks come from the [Phius 2024 Certification Guidebook](https://www.phius.org/phius-certification-guidebook),
§1.3.4.2 and §1.5.2.4. Code and manufacturer instructions also apply.

- Deliver outdoor air to every bedroom.
- Keep outdoor-air intakes away from exhausts, the garage and the range hood. Show clearances on the
  drawings.
- Use accessible filters, MERV 13 or better, with fans sized for the filter resistance.
- Insulate the outdoor-connected ERV ducts to at least R-8 and seal their vapor jackets.
- Place the ERV's general kitchen extract at least 6 ft from the cooktop and protect it from grease.
- Measure and balance every room before occupancy. Keep whole-house supply and extract within 10%, and
  test the hood and makeup air running together.
- Leave access for filter changes, grille cleaning and rebalancing.
