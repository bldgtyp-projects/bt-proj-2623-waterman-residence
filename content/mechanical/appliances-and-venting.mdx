### Kitchen exhaust

Electric cooking still produces particles, grease and moisture. Use a hood that exhausts outdoors and
covers the cooking surface, sized for the range and how the owners cook.

The hood is currently **624 CFM**, almost three times the ERV's high-speed airflow. Check whether a
lower-flow hood with good capture will do. Follow its listed mounting height and duct requirements; see
[HVI's kitchen guidance](https://www.hvi.org/HVIORG/assets/File/public/publications/HVIRangeHood_4Feb08.pdf).

Code makeup-air rules ([2021 IRC M1503.6](https://codes.iccsafe.org/content/IRC2021V3.0/chapter-15-exhaust-systems))
turn on fuel-burning appliances. Confirm the Pennsylvania amendments and Pittsburgh's reading. In a house
this tight, even a hood under 400 CFM can depressurize it.

### Makeup air

Whatever the hood and dryer exhaust has to come back in, and the balanced ERV won't supply it. The
mechanical designer should check exhaust fans that run together and the resulting house pressure, then
size an interlocked makeup-air system if needed.

Bring the air in where it won't disturb the hood or create a cold draft, with dampers, insulation and
condensation control. Whether to heat it depends on airflow and winter comfort; an electric resistance
heater is one option.

Commission the hood and makeup air together, with the other exhaust fans likely to run at the same
time. Record delivered airflow and house pressure.

### Other systems to resolve

- **Steam humidifier.** Check window and envelope condensation at **35% winter RH** before choosing
  equipment and controls. The ERV slows winter drying but cannot hold a humidity setpoint.
- **Dehumidifier.** The house needs moisture control when there is little cooling to do. Select it with
  the heat pumps. A stand-alone unit adds heat to the house, which matters most in an outage.
- **Hot-water recirculation.** Insulate the piping and use demand controls.
- **Outdoor heated slabs.** Confirm how the owners will use them. Snow-melt needs moisture and
  temperature controls; a heated screened porch needs a decision on operating hours.
- **Dryer.** Confirm type and exhaust.
- **Elevator.** Get the supplier's operating, standby and cab-lighting energy data.
