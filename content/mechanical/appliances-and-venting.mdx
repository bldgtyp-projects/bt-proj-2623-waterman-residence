### Kitchen exhaust

Electric cooking still produces particles, grease and moisture. Use a hood that exhausts outdoors and
covers the cooking surface, sized for the range and how the owners cook.

The hood currently assumed in the model is set to **624 CFM**, almost three times the ERV's high-speed airflow. Check whether a lower-flow hood with good capture will do. Follow its listed mounting height and duct requirements; see [HVI's kitchen guidance](https://www.hvi.org/HVIORG/assets/File/public/publications/HVIRangeHood_4Feb08.pdf).

Code makeup-air rules ([2021 IRC M1503.6](https://codes.iccsafe.org/content/IRC2021V3.0/chapter-15-exhaust-systems)) depend on whether the home includes fuel-burning appliances. Confirm final appliances against the Pennsylvania amendments and Pittsburgh's reading.

### Makeup air

Venting exhaust appliances (clothes dryers, kitchen hoods, etc.) in an airtight home may call for a dedicated makeup air system. That goes double for homes with indoor combustion appliances such as wood stoves and fireplaces, where an automatic makeup air fan balances the exhausted air.

Outdoor makeup air may need heating or cooling once it enters the habitable space. Include self-closing (magnetic) dampers on all ducting to stop air infiltration and exfiltration when the appliances are off. To hold comfort while makeup air is being supplied, size and install a high-output heating element to bring the incoming air up to temperature.

Two self-modulating makeup air systems (MUAS) with matching makeup air heaters (MUAH) would suit this project:

* [Electro Industries](https://electromn.com/make-up-air-2/)
* [Neptronic](https://www.neptronic.com/cmu/)

Commission the hood and makeup air together, with the other exhaust fans likely to run at the same
time.

### Other systems to resolve

- **Steam humidifier.** Due to low occupancy and large size, it is recommended to include a dedicated humidifier to help with winter-time dry air.
- **Dehumidifier.** A stand-alone dehumidification unit can help supplement the heat pumps during moderate cooling periods and help with controllability of the indoor air.
- **Hot-water recirculation.** Routing, tanks, lengths, size, controls.
- **Dryer.** Confirm final type and exhaust strategy.
