title: "Fresh-air ventilation"
---

The house needs controlled outdoor air with the windows closed. We recommend the architect's proposed
energy-recovery ventilator (ERV), running continuously on dedicated ducts. It brings in outdoor air and
removes stale air, transferring heat and some moisture between the two streams.

In winter, moisture recovery keeps the house from drying out. In summer it reduces the heat and moisture
coming in. **An ERV is not a dehumidifier.** The heat pumps or a separate unit must remove the rest.

<figure class="btwr-section-hero">
  <img src="/assets/mechanical/cascade-ventilation.png" alt="Outdoor air enters a bedroom, passes through a transfer opening and leaves through a bathroom extract grille." />
</figure>

Supply bedrooms and living spaces; extract from bathrooms, laundry rooms and a general kitchen point.
Door undercuts or transfer grilles let air through closed doors. The cooking hood is separate.

Size airflow to code and room needs, no higher. Extra airflow dries the house in winter and adds
moisture in summer. Check noise and pressure across closed doors.
