title: "Fresh-air ventilation"
---

Every home needs fresh air, even with the windows closed. We recommend a dedicated fresh-air 
energy-recovery ventilator (ERV), running continuously on its own ducts separate from the heating and cooling systems. This dedicated fresh-air unit brings in outdoor air and removes stale air 24/7 while passively transferring heat and some moisture between the two air streams in order to reduce energy demand and improve comfort.

In winter an ERV's moisture-recovery helps keep the house from drying out. In summer it helps reduces the outdoor moisture coming in. It is important to note that the ERV does not _remove_ humididty from the home. The home still
needs an active dehumidifcation system alonside the ERV to remove excess humidity.

Every room in the home is one of three kinds:

- **Supply** rooms receive fresh outdoor air from the ERV: bedrooms, living spaces, offices.
- **Extract** rooms have the stale air pulled out: bathrooms, laundry rooms, a general kitchen point,
  and any room that makes odors or fumes.
- **Transfer** air passes through in-between rooms on its way from supply rooms to
  extract rooms: halls, stairs, closets, the elevator shaft.
