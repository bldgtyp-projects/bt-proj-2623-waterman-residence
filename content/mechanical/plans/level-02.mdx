Supply air (blue) serves the primary bedroom and the office; extract (red) is drawn from the primary bathroom and WC, the guest bathroom and the laundry. The hall, stair, closets and elevator are transfer zones. Undercut or grille the doors between the primary bedroom, the closet and the bathroom so air can move through the suite.
