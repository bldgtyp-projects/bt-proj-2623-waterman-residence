Supply air (blue) serves the living room, the library and guest room, the sunroom and the hall; extract (red) is drawn from the kitchen and dining room, the accessible bathroom and the laundry and mudroom. The entry, stair and elevator are transfer zones. Place the kitchen extract at least 6 ft from the cooktop.
